# About Matkewaali

> Matkewaali is a handmade ceramics shop by Meghna Polimera, a self-taught ceramic artist based in Ballard, Seattle, WA.

I am Meghna Polimera, and my journey with clay began in 2019 through self-directed exploration. As a self-taught ceramic artist, I create functional wheel-thrown pieces that emphasize rustic aesthetics through varied textures and muted tones.

My work explores the natural qualities of clay and surface, combining techniques like hand-texturing, carving, and slip trailing to create pieces with tactile depth and organic character. I favor earthy palettes and raw finishes that bring a sense of warmth and authenticity to everyday objects.

The name Matkewaali is Hindi for "woman who sells ceramics." The icon — the Devanagari letter म, the Hindi equivalent of M — is my maker's mark, stamped into the bottom of every piece I make.

I also draw inspiration from Indian folk and tribal art traditions — including Warli, Gond, Sohrai, Kolam, Bandhani, and Kalamkari. These traditions, which have carried stories of communities for generations through bold geometries and nature-inspired motifs, translate beautifully onto ceramic surfaces. Through slip trailing and pattern work, I honor these visual languages while creating pieces that bridge ancestral patterns with contemporary functional design.

Being self-taught has shaped my approach through intuitive exploration and deliberate practice, leading to a style that feels both personal and connected to larger artistic traditions.
